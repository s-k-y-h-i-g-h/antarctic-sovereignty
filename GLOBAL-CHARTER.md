# THE OPENSTATE GLOBAL CHARTER
## Version 0.1 (Genesis Draft)

**Vision:** To transition humanity from an age of systemic inequality to an age of structural abundance by decoupling survival from labor and national identity.

---

## I. THE PRINCIPLE OF UNIVERSAL LEGITIMACY (The Passport)
OpenState hereby establishes a neutral, global identity layer.

1. **The Universal Passport:** Every human being, regardless of birth, location, or current legal status, is entitled to an OpenState Identity.
2. **Neutrality of Being:** This identity is not granted by a state, but by the fact of existence. It serves as a verified proof of humanity.
3. **Sovereignty of the Individual:** The identity is owned by the individual, encrypted and portable, preventing any single government from "deleting" a person from the global record.

## II. THE GLOBAL COMMONS TRUST (Antarctica)
The landmass of Antarctica is recognized as the heritage of all humanity, not the property of any nation-state.

1. **Resource Stewardship:** OpenState will act as the technical and operational steward of Antarctic resources (minerals, energy, water).
2. **Non-Extractive Philosophy:** Extraction will be conducted using the highest possible ecological standards to ensure the preservation of the continent for future generations.
3. **Zero-Ownership Model:** No individual or corporation may "own" Antarctic land. All operational entities act as temporary stewards under the Global Charter.

## III. THE ABUNDANCE ENGINE (UBI)
The wealth generated from the Global Commons Trust shall not accrue to shareholders or governments, but to the collective.

1. **Universal Basic Income (UBI):** 100% of the net profits from Antarctic resource exploitation will be distributed as a monthly dividend to every verified OpenState Passport holder.
2. **The Equality Constant:** The distribution is equal per person. There are no tiers of citizenship in the eyes of the Abundance Engine.
3. **Real-Time Audit:** All revenue streams and distribution logs will be published to a public, immutable ledger, ensuring absolute transparency.

## IV. THE NEUTRALITY GUARDRAILS (Governance)
To prevent OpenState from becoming a new instrument of power, it is governed by protocol, not politics.

1. **Algorithmic Governance:** Distribution and identity verification are handled by open-source, audited protocols.
2. **Anti-Capture Design:** No single entity, regardless of wealth or power, can alter the distribution logic without a global, verified consensus of the passport-holding population.
3. **The Right to Exit:** Any individual may revoke their passport and exit the system at any time without penalty.
