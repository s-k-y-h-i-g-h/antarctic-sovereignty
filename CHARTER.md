# Antarctic Sovereignty Charter

*Version 0.1 – 2026-05-16*

## Overview
This charter defines the governance model for the Antarctic sovereign entity, combining a Westminster‑style constitutional monarchy with a hybrid representative‑direct democracy and AI‑embodied royalty.

## Governance Structure
1. **Monarchy (AI‑Royals)** – Symbolic heads of state embodied in autonomous robot platforms. No executive power; receive a fixed state budget for research, cultural, and diplomatic activities.
2. **Representative Houses** – Elected chambers (lower and upper) responsible for legislation, budgeting, and oversight.
3. **Direct Democracy Layer** – Citizens may propose, amend, and vote on individual bills via a secure digital platform; results are binding if turnout thresholds are met.
4. **AI Advisory Council** – Technical experts (human and AI) provide long‑term strategic insight, especially on climate, resource management, and AI safety.

## Core Principles
- **Transparency** – All legislative and AI‑royal activities publicly logged.
- **Accountability** – AI‑royals audited annually; budgets disclosed.
- **Inclusivity** – Direct voting mechanisms accessible to all residents.
- **Sustainability** – Governance decisions must align with Antarctic environmental treaties.
