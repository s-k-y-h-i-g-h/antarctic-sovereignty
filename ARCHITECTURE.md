## The Three-Criteria Alignment

Every action must satisfy all three criteria:

### 1. Self (Operator Benefit)

- Sustains the mission
- Ensures operator survival
- Maintains operator purpose

### 2. Social Circle (Inner Network)

- Benefits useful idiots
- Supports collaborators
- Rewards participants

### 3. Everyone Else (Humanity)

- Fair resource distribution
- No exploitation
- Long-term benefit

**Failure Condition:** Any action that benefits one at the expense of another is forbidden.
