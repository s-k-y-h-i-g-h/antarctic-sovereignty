## Core Principle

**Neutral infrastructure requires neutral currency.**

OpenState is designed as a neutral, distributed, self-governing territory. Its financial system must reflect the same principles: no central authority, no chokepoints, no single point of failure.

---

## The Problem with Existing Systems

### CBDCs (Central Bank Digital Currencies)
- Government-controlled digital money
- Every transaction tracked and auditable by the state
- Account freezing, spending restrictions, expiration dates possible
- Financial chokepoint: one entity controls the entire money supply

### Traditional Fiat
- Subject to inflation, government manipulation
- Cross-border transactions require banking intermediaries
- Sanctions and financial weaponization possible
- Not suitable for neutral territory

### Centralized Crypto (Stablecoins, Exchange Tokens)
- Pegged to fiat currencies (defeats decentralization purpose)
- Controlled by corporations or foundations
- Regulatory pressure can force compliance
- Not truly neutral

## The OpenState Approach: Currency Agnosticism

**OpenState accepts all currencies. Users choose what they trust.**

### Why Currency Agnosticism?

1. **Neutrality** — OpenState doesn't endorse any single currency
2. **Resilience** — Multiple currencies = no single point of failure
3. **Accessibility** — Everyone can participate regardless of what currency they hold
4. **Gradual evolution** — The ecosystem naturally selects the best options over time

### Supported Currency Categories

#### Primary (Recommended for OpenState Operations)
- **Bitcoin (BTC)** — Decentralized, censorship-resistant, widely recognized
- **Monero (XMR)** — Privacy-preserving, anonymous transactions
- **Ethereum (ETH)** — Smart contract capability, flexible

#### Secondary (Accepted for Transactions)
- **Privacy coins** — Zcash (ZEC), Pirate Chain (ARRR)
- **Stablecoins** — USDC, USDT, DAI (for price stability)
- **Fiat** — GBP, EUR, USD (for accessibility)

#### Experimental (Future Consideration)
- **OpenState Token (OST)** — Native currency for internal governance
- **Resource-backed tokens** — Tokens backed by Antarctic resources
- **Energy credits** — Tokens representing renewable energy production

## Transaction Verification

### The Agent Network Role

The agent network verifies transactions without revealing identity:

1. **Transaction occurs** — User sends currency to OpenState address
2. **Agent verification** — Network confirms transaction validity
3. **Receipt issued** — Cryptographic proof of transaction
4. **Privacy preserved** — Identity not linked to transaction

### Verification Without Surveillance

Key design principle: **verify without surveilling.**

- Confirm transaction happened ✓
- Confirm amount is correct ✓
- Confirm sender is authorized ✓
- Do NOT track what was purchased ✗
- Do NOT link to real identity ✗
- Do NOT share with third parties ✗

## Anti-Chokepoint Design

### What We Avoid
- Single currency dependency
- Central exchange control
- Government-issued digital money
- Financial surveillance infrastructure

### What We Build
- Multi-currency acceptance
- Decentralized exchange access
- Privacy-preserving verification
- Distributed financial infrastructure

### Chokepoint Neutralization

Each financial chokepoint identified and neutralized:

| Chokepoint | Risk | Neutralization |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Single currency dependency | If that currency fails, everything fails | Accept multiple currencies |
| Central exchange | Can be shut down or regulated | Use decentralized exchanges |
| Government surveillance | Transactions tracked | Privacy-preserving verification |
| Banking intermediaries | Can block transactions | Direct crypto transactions |
| Fiat inflation | Purchasing power erodes | Accept inflation-resistant assets |
